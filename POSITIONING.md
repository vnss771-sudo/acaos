# ACAOS Positioning Strategy

## Overview

ACAOS is repositioned as a multi-module revenue platform with **Inbox Assistant** as the primary entry-level offer, informed by the proven GTM strategy from X10THINK's paid-pilot business model.

## Primary Offer: Inbox Assistant

### Target Customer
- Digital agencies (10-50 person teams)
- Service providers needing efficient email workflow
- Professionals managing high-volume inbox triage

### Key Value Propositions
1. **AI-powered email intelligence** — Classify, summarize, and recommend actions on incoming email
2. **Smart reply composition** — Generate contextually aware responses in your voice
3. **Inbox automation** — Reduce manual triage and response time
4. **Opportunity detection** — Flag leads and opportunities in your inbox patterns

### Differentiators
- **Human-in-the-loop** — All AI suggestions require human approval before sending (trust-building)
- **No auto-send by default** — Conservative, audit-friendly approach for professional users
- **Multi-tenant ready** — Agencies can use for multiple client inboxes
- **Fully self-serve** — No waiting for support; immediate ROI

## Pricing Model

### Inbox Assistant (Starter Plan)
- **Monthly:** $150/month + setup fee
- **Setup includes:** 30 min onboarding call, inbox configuration, team seat setup
- **Included:** Email integration, AI research/composition, inbox management
- **Limits:** 300 AI calls/month, up to 10,000 leads

### Growth Plan (Enterprise)
- **Custom pricing** for teams needing:
  - Multiple workspaces and unlimited seats
  - Dedicated success manager
  - Custom integrations and SLAs
  - Advanced discovery and outreach (Missions module)
